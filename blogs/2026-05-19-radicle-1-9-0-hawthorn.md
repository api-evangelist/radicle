The Radicle team would like to announce the release of Radicle 1.9.0, code name Hawthorn . The Hawthorn tree, or Mayflower, signifies the coming of summer, and has significance for many cultures. In particular, the Hawthorn tree represents the door to the “other side” and is the home of the fairies in Irish culture.
